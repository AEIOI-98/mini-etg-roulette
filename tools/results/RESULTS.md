## Wheel frequency check

Pearson chi-square = **39.909041**, 36 degrees of freedom; expected 270270.270270 observations per pocket. The upper-tail 1% critical value is 58.619000. Uniformity is not rejected at the 1% level.

A non-rejection is not proof of randomness, independence or hardware fairness. The test does not measure serial correlation. A genuine uniform source can exceed the threshold in roughly 1% of repeated experiments.
